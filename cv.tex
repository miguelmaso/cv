\section{CV Summary}
Civil Engineer and PhD in Computational Mechanics. I write and maintain research software in C++, Python and Julia, and have taken engineering software from internal prototypes to tools in daily use.

At CIMNE, first as PhD candidate and now as postdoctoral researcher, I develop numerical formulations and implement them in open-source frameworks (Kratos Multiphysics, HyperFEM.jl), where I also review contributions and guide other developers.

As Head of Development and Innovation at SOCOTEC Spain (2022--2025) I defined and delivered software connecting calculation models with BIM platforms, turned requests from engineering teams into scoped deliverables and supervised a remote developer, while leading simulation work on international building projects.
