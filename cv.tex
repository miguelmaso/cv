\section{CV Summary}
Civil Engineer and PhD in Computational Mechanics. Doctoral research on Fluid-Structure Interaction and coupled Eulerian-Lagrangian methods for wave propagation and impact, published in \emph{Communications in Nonlinear Science and Numerical Simulation} and \emph{Advanced Modeling and Simulation in Engineering Sciences}. Background in FEM, CFD and shallow-water / free-surface flow modelling, combined with production-level scientific programming in C++, Python and Julia, including parallel implementations run on HPC clusters.

Industrial application includes a CFD wind-loading analysis on a high-rise facade (Arup) and coupled CFD-structural simulations of thermo-plastic failure under fire loads (SOCOTEC France), carried out while leading development and innovation projects at SOCOTEC Spain — building tools for interoperability between calculation models and BIM software. Currently postdoctoral researcher at CIMNE, developing constitutive formulations for new materials.
