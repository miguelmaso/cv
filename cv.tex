\section{Research profile}
Civil Engineer and PhD in computational mechanics, with research experience since 2017 in finite element methods, multiphysics coupling and the development of scientific software (Julia, C++, Python). My doctoral work at CIMNE addressed stabilised finite element formulations and the coupling of Lagrangian and Eulerian solvers for free-surface flows and fluid--structure interaction (particle finite element method, shallow water equations).

As postdoctoral researcher I work on thermodynamically consistent constitutive frameworks for electro-active polymers at finite strains, and on their implementation in Julia, including HyperFEM.jl, a distributed finite element library built on Gridap.jl and designed to run on Linux clusters.

This research profile is complemented by three years of industrial experience applying large-scale numerical simulation and software automation to real structures, and by university teaching in structural dynamics and numerical methods at UPC.
